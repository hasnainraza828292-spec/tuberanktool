YouTube hashtag rules to remember

  • Hashtags must start with # and contain no spaces.
  • YouTube shows up to 3 hashtags above your video title.
  • If a video has more than 15 hashtags, YouTube ignores all of them.
  • Only use hashtags that truly relate to your video.

Hashtag generator FAQ

Where do I put hashtags?

In your video description or title. Hashtags in the description appear above the title (the first three).

Are hashtags the same as tags?

No. Tags are hidden metadata in YouTube Studio. Hashtags are visible, clickable labels.

Will hashtags increase my views?

No tool can guarantee that. Relevant hashtags can help viewers find related videos, but they are a small piece of the picture.
