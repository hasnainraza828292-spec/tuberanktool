YouTube Keyword Idea Generator

Start with one seed phrase and get dozens of video-topic ideas grouped by type. This is a brainstorming helper: it does not show search volume or competition. To see what real viewers search for, use the "Check" link beside each idea to open it on YouTube.

How to find the best ideas from this list

  1. Click Check beside an idea. YouTube will show the results and its own search suggestions.
  2. Look at the top videos. Are they recent? Do they answer the question well? Could you explain it better or more simply?
  3. Choose ideas where you can add something genuinely useful, then use them in your title, description and tags.

The "alphabet prompts" are a manual trick: type your phrase followed by a letter into the YouTube search box and see what it auto-suggests. Those suggestions come from real searches.

Keyword tool FAQ

Why no search volume numbers?

Reliable volume data comes from paid sources. Showing invented numbers would be misleading, so this free tool leaves them out.

Are all these phrases real searches?

Not necessarily. They are phrasing patterns that often match how people search. Use the Check link to see what YouTube actually returns.

Can I use these as video topics?

Yes, as long as you can make a genuinely helpful video about them.
