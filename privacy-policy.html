Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Summary

TubeRank Tools is a static website. The tools run entirely in your browser, and the text you type into them is not sent to us or stored by the site.

Information we do not collect

The site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no database, and no code that collects personal information. At the time of writing, the site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising scripts.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses when pages are requested. See GitHub's own privacy statement for details.

Contacting us

If you email us through the contact page, we receive whatever you choose to send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to reply.

External links

Some pages link to YouTube search results. When you click those links, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Advertising

The site contains clearly labelled, optional ad placeholders. If real advertising is added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what cookies or identifiers third-party advertisers may use and how you can opt out.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
