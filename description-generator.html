YouTube Description Generator

A good description tells viewers and YouTube what your video covers. Fill in the details you already know and this tool arranges them into a tidy layout with your summary first, then key points, timestamps, links and a closing line.

Replace the [mm:ss] placeholders with real times. YouTube chapters need the first timestamp at 00:00 and at least three timestamps in order. Delete the timestamp section if you don't want chapters.

What makes a useful description

Only the first couple of lines show before viewers click "Show more", so the most important information belongs at the top. Write for people first: explain what the video covers, what they will learn and where to go next. Avoid stuffing in unrelated keywords, as that makes your video look untrustworthy and can break YouTube's spam policies.

Description generator FAQ

How long can a description be?

Up to 5,000 characters. Most descriptions work well at a fraction of that.

Does the tool write the summary for me?

No. It uses the sentence you provide, so the description stays accurate and sounds like you.

Where do I paste the description?

In YouTube Studio, open your video's Details page and paste into the Description box.
