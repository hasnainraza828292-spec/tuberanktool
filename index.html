How these tools work (and what they don't do)

Every tool on this site runs inside your web browser using simple, transparent rules: your words are combined with common phrasing patterns that viewers actually use when they search, such as "how to", "for beginners" and "tips". Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is generated by a hidden AI.

That also means we are honest about the limits. We do not show search volume, competition scores or ranking predictions, because a free static website has no access to that data. Treat the results as a fast starting point. Read them, edit them, and keep only what truly matches your video.

YouTube SEO basics in plain language

Say clearly what your video is about. YouTube and viewers both rely on your title, thumbnail and description to understand your video. A specific title beats a clever but vague one.

Put the important words first. Titles are often shortened in search results, and only the first lines of a description are visible before "Show more".

Be realistic about tags. YouTube has said tags play a small role in discovery. They can help with common misspellings, but they are not a shortcut to ranking. Time spent on a great title and a useful video usually pays off more.

Yes. The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript hosted for free. There are no accounts, subscriptions or hidden limits.
