YouTube Tag Generator

Type your video topic and get a ready-to-paste, comma-separated tag list. The tags are built from your own words plus common viewer phrasing like "for beginners" and "how to", and trimmed to fit YouTube's limit.

How to use the tags

  1. Enter the main subject of your video, in the words a viewer would type.
  2. Add any extra keywords that are specific to your video.
  3. Click Generate tags, then delete anything that doesn't describe your video.
  4. Click Copy tags and paste them into the Tags box under "Show more" in YouTube Studio.

The list is kept under 450 characters so it stays safely inside YouTube's 500-character tag limit. Tags are a minor signal: your title, thumbnail and content matter more.

Tag generator FAQ

Do YouTube tags still matter?

YouTube says tags play a small role in how videos are found. They can help when viewers commonly misspell a word, but your title, thumbnail and description are more important.

How many characters can tags use?

YouTube allows up to 500 characters in total across all tags for a video.

Are these tags based on real search data?

No. They are built from your input and common phrasing patterns. This tool does not show search volume or ranking predictions.

Should I use every tag the tool suggests?

No. Keep only the tags that honestly describe your video. Misleading tags can violate YouTube's policies.
